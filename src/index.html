Engineering notes

Ice-safe sliding base
  • UHMW-PE runners/skids: low friction on ice, non-marking, tough in the cold. No rubber (grabs, scuffs) or bare metal edges (cuts).
  • Radiused or beveled leading edges; fasteners counterbored from the top so no metal touches the ice.
  • ~250 lb over the runners is a few psi — far below a skate blade. Add a perimeter drip lip.
Freezer integration · Meling DW-XL90
  • It's a freezer (−15 to −30 °C): beer stored inside would freeze and burst. Use it as an ice / cold-pack reserve, or swap to a same-size +2–8 °C medical refrigerator.
  • Front-ventilated: door grille must line up with the bottom-front vent. ~1″ behind for the cord.
  • The enclosure is a sleeve that drops over the freezer and pins to the sled.
  • Confirm what the "48 hr backup battery" powers — often alarm/display only, not the compressor.
  • Transport upright; let it stand before power-up per Meling.
Cup / cooler
  • Double wall: decorative shell around a stainless or food-grade liner, foam-filled to cut sweating.
  • Melt water drains through the neck to a sealed reservoir — never onto the freezer or ice.
  • Twist-lock mount on the enclosure cap; carry it on empty, load beer + ice on the ice.
Getting it onto the ice
  • Three lifts, not one: freezer + sled by cart, sleeve by two people, cup by one.
  • Check on-ice equipment rules and approvals with the venue and league before fabrication.
Design language

Inspired by the luxury sports-object approach associated with Victor Solomon — stained glass, mirror chrome and gold, sports icons scaled into precious objects. Forms are original, not copies of his pieces.

Visual concept, not a fabrication model or a venue-approved installation. Weights and clearances are estimates and require technical validation. Brand marks are placeholders until approved artwork is uploaded.
